# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, students, lab builders, and workshop organizers exploring open-source biofabrication and liquid-handling systems. They need to scan the field quickly, understand what is actually reproducible, and move from paper-level discovery to the practical artifacts behind each system.

## Product Purpose

This product turns the current corpus of spreadsheets, PDFs, and PageIndex outputs into a lightweight public knowledge interface. It exists to make the ecosystem legible: which tools exist, which papers describe them, which repos or assets are available, and which systems are realistic for replication, teaching, or adaptation. Success means a user can move from a question such as "show me workshop-ready open-source bioprinters with downloadable PDFs" to a small, trustworthy set of evidence-backed records without digging through raw files.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, practical, connected. The interface should feel like an expert research map rather than a generic publication database: calm, precise, and useful, with enough visual character to make browsing feel intentional rather than bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

Avoid looking like a corporate SaaS dashboard with heavy chrome, a generic blog template, or a flat bibliography dump with no relationships. Avoid dark-pattern search UI, overly decorative science imagery, and overbuilt graph visualizations that obscure the records themselves.

## Design Principles

- Make evidence easy to verify.
- Show relationships before volume.
- Keep expert workflows fast.
- Reward scanning, then support depth.
- Preserve a path from paper to practice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Core navigation, filtering, and reading must work with keyboard-only use and without relying on motion. Use strong contrast, clear focus states, and category cues that do not depend on color alone. Support reduced-motion preferences and keep the site usable on low-powered devices and mobile screens.
