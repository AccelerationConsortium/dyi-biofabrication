# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, makers, educators, and students use the Atlas to locate practical biofabrication evidence, assess build feasibility, and move between papers, tools, assets, skills, and repositories without losing context.

## Product Purpose

DIY Biofabrication Atlas is a navigable research corpus for open-source biofabrication. It makes evidence, implementation details, and connected build records easier to discover and compare.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and open. The interface should feel like a well-maintained research instrument: focused enough for repeated use, but welcoming to readers with different levels of technical experience.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative visual noise, and permanently exposed control panels that compete with the evidence. Do not turn the corpus into a marketing site.

## Design Principles

- Put the current research task ahead of decoration.
- Reveal detail progressively; preserve context while moving through related records.
- Make complex evidence easy to scan, compare, and return to.
- Treat accessibility and legibility as core research infrastructure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both themes. Respect reduced-motion preferences, use visible keyboard focus, and ensure icon-only controls have text labels for assistive technology.
